Driftplain

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Driftplain is a maintained application operated by Steve Levit. It helps you choose an AI model for CI tasks and inspect reported usage, estimated costs and task results.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and an internal account ID. Password accounts store a password hash, rather than the password itself.
  • Google sign-in: Google sends a signed identity token, which may include your email, name and profile picture. Driftplain verifies the token and stores your Google account identifier and email to create or recognize your account. We do not store your Google name, picture or identity token, request your Google password, or access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.
  • Workspace information: projects, selected models, CI configuration, review preferences, submitted run results and findings, token usage, calculated costs, quality feedback, chat questions, answers and retrieval records. Uploaded catalog source documents are stored separately in object storage.
  • Operational information: request and error logs, network information such as IP addresses, and performance and model-usage metrics used to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used and shared

We use account information to sign you in and keep your workspace associated with your account. We use project and run data to provide benchmark exploration, CI setup, usage and quality dashboards, and grounded chat answers.

Google provides the sign-in service. The Google button loads resources from Google, so your browser communicates with Google on the sign-in and registration screens. Google's handling of that interaction is covered by its privacy policy.

The application and its database run on a server operated by Steve Levit in Israel. Traffic reaches it through Cloudflare, which proxies requests and therefore sees connection details such as IP addresses. Encrypted database backups and catalog source documents are stored in Amazon Web Services object storage in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1). The in-app assistant is currently offline on this deployment, so chat questions are not sent to an AI provider; if it is enabled, chat questions and catalog ingestion documents would be processed by Amazon Bedrock.

The CI agent runs in your own CI environment using your provider key. It sends code and instructions to the model provider you choose, under that provider's policies. Driftplain receives the run results you submit, including findings that may contain code details. Keep credentials in your CI credential store and avoid submitting confidential information to this service.

Driftplain does not sell your personal information, use Google account information for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Google account identifiers are used for authentication, not included in model prompts.

Browser storage and security

Driftplain stores its own session token in your browser's local storage so you can stay signed in. Signing out removes that local token. Google credentials and temporary sign-in challenges are held in memory for the sign-in attempt; the server records used challenges to prevent replay and clears expired records during later sign-ins.

The service uses HTTPS, password hashing and account-scoped access checks. Google-only accounts have no Driftplain password, and matching email addresses do not automatically link a Google account to an existing password account.

Retention and deletion requests

Account and workspace records are kept until you ask for their removal or the operator removes them. There is no automatic account-expiry schedule and no self-service account-deletion screen. Operational logs and backup copies may have different lifetimes from active application records.

To request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated data, email stevelevit230@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify ownership and will explain what can be removed, including any remaining backup copies. Do not send passwords or tokens.

You can also remove Driftplain's Google connection in your Google account settings. Removing that connection or signing out does not itself delete your Driftplain data.
